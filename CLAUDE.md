# Cue

Cue is a client agreement and electronic-signing service for photographers and
videographers, shipping as a Krevo subdomain at `cue.krevo.io`.

The repository-wide engineering and product instructions live in
[`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md). The business, marketing, and architecture spec lives
in [`solution.md`](./solution.md). Treat those files, live code, configuration,
migrations, and tests as authoritative.

Keep this file lean. Do not copy release history or tool-specific workflow rules
back into it.
