Cue — internal roadmap
======================

Internal only. Nothing here is promised publicly: the pricing page, FAQ, and
terms deliberately name no future features (removed 2026-08-08 — a card full
of "(planned)" sold the roadmap instead of the product). When something below
ships, move it into the pricing card copy and the changelog; don't re-add
"planned" verbiage to the public site.

One deliberate exception (2026-08-08): cloud storage. The rename strip and the
#storage section announce it for this fall, and the terms page carries the
"dates can move" caveat. That is the only future claim the site makes.

Announced publicly
------------------
- Cloud storage returns inside Cue, this fall (Krevo's original product;
  landing #storage section + topbar strip)

Pro
---
- Saved templates (also referenced in the FAQ answer about custom terms)
- Email reminders (nudge an unsigned party)

Studio
------
- Multiple users per studio
- Shared templates across the studio
- Custom domain for signing links
- Priority support

Platform
--------
- Stripe billing (the gate for everything above; plans are provisioned on the
  invite until then — see docs/solution.md "Launch billing decision")
- PDF export of the sealed record (terms currently disclaim this explicitly)
